How does this work?
  1. You pay. Your money goes into a safe, not to them yet.
  2. They do the work and tell you it’s done.
  3. You approve. Then they get paid, in naira if they like.
  4. Not happy? You can dispute before it’s paid.

The safe is a public smart contract on Celo, so nobody can quietly move your money. Fee: 1%. You pay in dollars (USD₮), so you need a wallet app like MiniPay with dollars in it.
